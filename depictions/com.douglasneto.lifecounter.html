MTG Life

Life counter for Magic

Features

  • 2 to 6 players, with panels facing each side of the table
  • Poison and commander damage per opponent
  • Starting life 20/25/30/40, pick who goes first
  • The game is saved and the screen stays on while playing

Compatibility

Jailbroken iOS 8 to 10, 32-bit devices (armv7). Tested on an iPad 3 running iOS 8.4.1.

Source code and license

Open source: github.com/douugr/vida-mtg
