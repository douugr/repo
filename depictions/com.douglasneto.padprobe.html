PadProbe

Bluetooth controller diagnostics

Features

  • Searches for and connects Bluetooth controllers through BTstack
  • Shows the HID reports in hex and highlights what changed
  • Handy to find out how a new controller talks to the iPad

Bluetooth controllers: the BTstack package is installed with it. Before using it, turn off iOS Bluetooth in Settings and keep Wi-Fi on (on some iPads both share the same chip).

Compatibility

Jailbroken iOS 8 to 10, 32-bit devices (armv7). Tested on an iPad 3 running iOS 8.4.1.
